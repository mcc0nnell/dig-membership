02 / Domain separation

Verification isn't permission

A verified identity is not an active membership, opt-in, election ballot or conference admission. These are separate decisions enforced server-side.

Member and attendee rules / independent paths

Sign-in does not activate membership, approve a campaign, expose a staff case or grant a ticket. No real-world email, checkout, check-in or ballot service is enabled by these diagrams.

03 / Operational journeys

Four journeys. Different permissions.

Based on the updated offline walkthroughs and current development contracts. The diagrams remain source-readable when third-party rendering is unavailable.

NTC · verified paid admission

Purchase is not admission

sequenceDiagram
    autonumber
    actor Attendee
    participant Nexus as DIG Nexus
    participant Neon as Neon PostgreSQL
    participant Zeffy as Zeffy hosted checkout
    participant Staff as Authorized event staff
    Note over Attendee,Staff: Staging contract only - live sales and expiry jobs off
    Attendee->>Nexus: Start registration (no membership required)
    Nexus->>Neon: Record non-holding payment request
    Nexus-->>Attendee: Zeffy hosted checkout
    Attendee->>Zeffy: Pay on provider page
    Zeffy-->>Nexus: Signed event notification
    Nexus->>Neon: Validate purchase and atomically claim seat
    alt Valid paid receipt and available seat
      Neon-->>Nexus: Verified admission
      Nexus-->>Attendee: Scoped confirmation without accommodation data
      Attendee->>Staff: Present scoped QR or manual confirmation
      Staff->>Nexus: Authorized check-in
    else Mismatch, refund, late payment or sold out
      Neon-->>Nexus: Restricted manual review
      Nexus-->>Staff: Review required, no admission granted
    end

Non-holding requests do not decrement inventory. Payment-provider success alone is never sufficient for admission. Example behavior only: no payment or ticket is created here.
