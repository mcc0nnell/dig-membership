02 / Domain separation

Verification isn't permission

A verified identity is not an active membership, opt-in, election ballot or conference admission. These are separate decisions enforced server-side.

Member and attendee rules / independent paths

flowchart LR
    Verified["Verified identity"] --> Pending["Pending membership request"]
    Pending --> Review["Authorized policy approval"]
    Review --> Active["Active member"]
    Active --> Snapshot["Eligibility snapshot
not ballot casting"]
    Optin["Explicit newsletter consent"] --> Subscribed["Mailing eligibility"]
    Directory["Separate directory opt-in"] --> Private["Scoped listing"]
    Access["Accommodation request"] --> Restricted["Restricted event staff"]
    Visitor["Nonmember attendee"] --> Event["NTC registration"]
    Event --> Payment["Zeffy payment verification"]
    Payment --> Checkin["Scoped admission / check-in"]

This is a logical policy diagram, not a claim that real-member enrollment, email, payment or check-in is enabled.
