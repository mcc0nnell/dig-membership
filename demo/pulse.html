02 / Trust is the product

The visualizations move. The boundaries don't.

This presentation is built to explain policy-sensitive operations to people, not turn fictional numbers into official statistics.

  • Membership: verified identity and active membership are distinct authorization outcomes.
  • NTC / DC27: non-holding requests do not reserve stock or issue tickets. Only verified, server-authorized admissions allocate capacity.
  • Communications: revoked or pending consent is never eligible for simulated delivery; all actual sends remain at zero.
  • Access: all essential values are available in labeled tables and native keyboard-operated controls, with reduced-motion support.
